# AbsForge – 30 Day Abs Workout

## Product Vision
A premium Android fitness app focused on building core strength through a structured 30-day no-equipment home workout program.

## Core Screens
1. **Onboarding**: Intro, Goals, Level, Personal Stats.
2. **Home Dashboard**: Today's workout, streak, progress ring, quick stats.
3. **30 Day Program**: Calendar view of all days with locked/unlocked states.
4. **Workout Overview**: Exercise list, duration, difficulty before starting.
5. **Active Workout**: Large exercise visual, rep/timer, coaching text.
6. **Rest Timer**: Countdown, next exercise preview, banner ad placeholder.
7. **Workout Complete**: Stats, rewards, sharing, progress update.
8. **Exercise Library**: Searchable list by category (Upper, Lower, Obliques).
9. **Exercise Details**: Instructions, animations, common mistakes.
10. **Progress**: Analytics, streak, activity charts, weight progress.
11. **Body Tracker**: Weight, BMI, body measurements.
12. **Daily Reminder**: Notification settings and time selector.
13. **Profile**: Settings, subscription status, personal info.
14. **Workout Settings**: Rest duration, voice coach, sounds.
15. **Premium / Pro**: Subscription tiers and benefits.

## Design Principles
- **Fast Path to Training**: 1-2 taps to start the day's workout.
- **High Retention**: Visible streaks and progress rings.
- **Distraction-Free**: No ads during active exercise.
- **Premium Aesthetic**: Dark mode, lime green accents, high-quality illustrations.
