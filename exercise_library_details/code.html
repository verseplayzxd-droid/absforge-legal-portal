LOWER ABS BEGINNER

Reverse Crunch

A fundamental movement for targeting the lower abdominal region and building core stability.

format_list_numbered Execution

  • 1

    Lie on your back with arms extended by your sides, palms facing down for support.

  • 2

    Bend your knees at 90 degrees and raise your legs so your thighs are perpendicular to the floor.

  • 3

    Exhale and contract your lower abs to curl your hips off the floor, bringing your knees toward your chest.

  • 4

    Inhale and slowly lower your hips back to the starting position with control.

warning Common Mistake

Using momentum to swing the legs rather than engaging the core to lift the pelvis.

lightbulb Pro Tip

Imagine trying to touch your knees to the ceiling, lifting upward instead of backward.
